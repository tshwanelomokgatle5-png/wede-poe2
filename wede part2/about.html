Organisation Overview

Fresh Bakes Bakery is a local bakery that sells freshly baked bread, cakes, muffins, biscuits, pies, and pastries. The bakery serves the surrounding community by providing affordable, high-quality baked goods made fresh every day.

Mission

To provide fresh, delicious, and affordable baked products while delivering excellent customer service.

Vision

To become the most trusted and preferred bakery in the local community.

Who We Serve (Target Audience)

  • Families
  • Students
  • Office workers
  • Event planners
  • Coffee lovers
